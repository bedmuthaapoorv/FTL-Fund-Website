Investing involves risk, including the possible loss of the entire amount invested.

- **Market risk.** The value of investments, and any income from them, can fall as well as rise.
- **Model and process risk.** A systematic, rules-based process can fail to perform as intended. Rules developed and tested on historical data may not behave the same way in future market conditions.
- **Concentration and liquidity risk.** Positions may be concentrated and may not be possible to exit at the expected price or time.
- **Operational risk.** Errors in data, software, execution or administration can cause losses.
- **Key-person risk.** The process depends on a small number of individuals [PLACEHOLDER].
- **Regulatory risk.** Changes in law, regulation or taxation may adversely affect the fund or its investors.

Past performance is not indicative of future results. No assurance can be given that any investment objective will be achieved.
